Our Mission

Building confidence, one connection at a time

We believe girlhood is worth celebrating. Our programs pair mentorship, creativity, and community so every girl grows up knowing she belongs.

🤝

Mentorship

One-on-one and group mentoring that helps girls set goals and build lasting confidence.

🎉

Community Events

Workshops, meetups, and celebrations that bring girls and families together year-round.

🌱

Growth & Leadership

Hands-on programming that builds real-world skills, leadership, and self-expression.

Why We Exist

A space to be seen, heard, and celebrated

Girlhood Collective started as a small circle of mentors in Cincinnati and has grown into a network of volunteers, families, and partner organizations committed to girls' well-being.

From after-school programming to summer gatherings, every initiative is designed with one goal: helping girls feel confident in who they are.
