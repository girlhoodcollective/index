About Us

A collective built by and for girls

We started with a simple belief: girls thrive when they have community, mentors, and space to grow into themselves. Here's our story so far.

Our Story

From a living room circle to a citywide community

Girlhood Collective began as a handful of Cincinnati mentors meeting with a small group of girls after school. What started as informal check-ins grew into structured programming, community events, and a network of families and partner organizations across the city.

Today, we're proud to support hundreds of girls each year through mentorship, workshops, and celebrations that center their voices and growth.
