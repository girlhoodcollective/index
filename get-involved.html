Volunteer

Become a mentor

Mentors meet weekly or monthly with a small group of girls, offering consistency, encouragement, and a listening ear. No special experience required — just a willingness to show up.

  • Background check and orientation provided
  • Flexible scheduling around school-year programming
  • Ongoing training and mentor community support
